# Invoicium Design Brief

Review the entire Invoicium app and make the design feel completely unified, premium, and production-ready across every page, component, and screen size. The goal is a 10/10 modern business SaaS product that feels polished, trustworthy, expensive, and consistent — not like disconnected screens or a template.

Be very careful with credit/token usage. Do not rewrite the entire app unnecessarily. First inspect the existing structure, identify the highest-impact design inconsistencies, then make targeted improvements only where needed. Preserve working functionality and avoid touching backend logic unless required for layout or UI consistency.

Focus on:
- consistent spacing, padding, margins, border radius, shadows, typography, and colors
- unified buttons, cards, forms, tables, modals, navigation, sidebars, headers, and empty states
- clean mobile responsiveness and desktop responsiveness
- fixing awkward spacing, alignment issues, overflow, cramped sections, random gaps, weird wrapping, inconsistent widths, and broken layouts
- making dashboards, invoices, quotes, jobs, clients, settings, and onboarding feel like one cohesive product
- ensuring every page looks professional on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- improving visual hierarchy so important actions are obvious
- keeping the interface smooth, clean, fast, and contractor-friendly

Do not make random cosmetic changes just for the sake of change. Keep the existing Invoicium brand identity, logo, colors, and overall direction, but refine it to feel much more premium. Use a consistent design system across the app: spacing scale, text sizes, card style, button variants, form fields, page headers, and responsive behavior.

Before editing, briefly audit the UI and list what you plan to fix. Then apply the improvements in the most efficient way possible. After changes, check for mobile and desktop issues, especially spacing, alignment, navigation behavior, scroll problems, and inconsistent component styling.

The final result should feel like a serious SaaS platform built for contractors — clean, powerful, polished, and ready for real customers.
